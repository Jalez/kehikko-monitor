Kind: feature

- **Is the connection good?** The Network chart now says whether your internet connection is Good, Fair, Poor or Offline, with the round trip and loss, such as "Good · 24 ms · 0% loss", and why when it is not, such as "high latency to gitlab.example.com: 420 ms". Monitor checks the hosts Kehikot really uses and one public address every 10 seconds, only while it is open.
- **Latency in the same chart.** The round trip runs as a dotted line beside download and upload. In a small container the head shows the verdict and the round trip over the sparkline.
- **Each host, and a speed test.** With room, each host's round trip and loss is listed under the chart, with a Test speed button. It downloads about 25 MB and uploads about 10 MB from Cloudflare, only when you press it, and you can cancel it.
- **In the footer and the Inbox.** The footer says "Connection good · 24 ms" beside the speeds. A connection that stays Poor or Offline for over a minute is an Inbox item until it recovers.

For agents: the `health` tool's `network` view now starts with the connection's verdict and reason, each host's round trip, loss, jitter and name lookup, and the last speed test. Ask it before a long push, pull or download.
