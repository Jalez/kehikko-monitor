- **Your GitHub account's real limits.** When gh is signed in, Monitor shows how much of your account's hourly limit is left and when it resets, for regular calls and for GraphQL points.
- **Kehikot's own share, in plain numbers.** Kehikot now keeps to a share of your account's limit, so your own gh, CI and other apps keep the rest; Monitor shows how much of that share it has used.
- **Paused, said in words.** When Kehikot is holding its GitHub reads, because its share is used, your account is nearly out, or GitHub asked it to wait, Monitor says why and until when.
- **The without-an-account budget only when it matters.** It is shown under its own plain name only when Kehikot actually read GitHub without an account.
