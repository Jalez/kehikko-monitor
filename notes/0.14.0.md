Kind: breaking

- **Disk moved to Worktrees.** Health no longer measures the disk: the Disk tile, the list of what Kehikot uses on the disk, the nearly-full-disk warning in the footer and the `disk` view of the health tool are gone. What your worktrees take, and cleaning them up, is now the Worktrees module's, with its own charts. Health keeps CPU and Memory, side by side.

## For agents

- The `health` tool no longer has a `disk` view (asking for it says where it went) and `now` no longer says free disk. Use the Worktrees module's `worktrees` tool for what worktrees take on the disk.
