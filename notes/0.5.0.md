- **Health, beside Traffic.** Choose View › Health to see how heavy and how well Kehikot and each module run on this computer: CPU and memory, how long their calls take and how many fail, how often each was started and whether it crashed, and what takes the most disk.
- **At a glance.** Five tiles — CPU, memory, calls, restarts and disk — each with its number in words and a small chart; as the container grows, the modules worth a look, a table of every running module and the biggest folders, agent worktrees with node_modules included.
- **The worst module in the footer.** The footer now also names the module most worth a look (or the busiest, when nothing is), the total CPU and memory, and the disk when it is nearly full, so a folded container still says it.
- **Agents can ask what is slow or heavy.** A new `health` tool answers with the worst first, then by CPU and memory, call times, restarts or disk.
- **Light on your computer.** Monitor looks only while its page is open or an agent asks, reads process figures and folder sizes but never what files hold, and keeps five minutes of samples in memory.
