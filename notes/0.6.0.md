- **Health reads well in one narrow column.** Each tile puts its title and number in a row of their own above the chart, so nothing is hidden under the bars, and a tile 260 pixels or wider adds a scale and the first and last time.
- **Charts say their numbers.** Point at a chart, touch it, or Tab to it and use the arrow keys: a small tooltip says the value against its limit and when, such as "134% CPU, 1.3 of 10 cores" or a module's slowest calls and starts.
- **Disk as a doughnut.** The disk tile shows what Kehikot takes, one segment each for its database, installed modules, logs, agent worktrees and the rest, named with its size beside the doughnut or in its tooltip, and the biggest in the middle when it fits.
