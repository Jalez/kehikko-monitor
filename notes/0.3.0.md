- **Every caller has a name.** Calls Kehikot receives say who sent them: a module, a Claude or Codex session and the kehikko and epic it is on, Kehikot itself, or a program by its own name, like "curl, from your terminal (zsh)". "An unknown program" is gone.
- **Sessions under their agent.** A module's sessions are counted under it, as in "Claude: 3 sessions", in the chart, its legend and the table of callers.
- **How Kehikot knew.** Open a call to see the program that made it, the program that started that one, and how Kehikot told who it was.
- **Lines, bars or area.** The timeline is a line for each caller by default, with free calls dashed. Choose Chart in the header to switch back to stacked bars, or to a stacked area. Monitor remembers your choice.
- **Readable when narrow.** In a narrow container, the lines show the five busiest callers and fold the rest into "Other callers".
