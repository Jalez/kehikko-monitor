- **Tooltips you can read.** A chart's tooltip is now solid, in larger type, with a clear border, and says at most three short lines: the figure first in bold, then whose it is, then the time. It sits above or below the chart, never over its labels.
- **Health takes less room.** The tiles lose their card borders and the gaps between them, with a thin line between tiles instead, and the charts run edge to edge. In one column each tile puts its title, its line in words and its number on one row, with the chart right under it.
- **The disk fits.** Its doughnut's row is as tall as the list beside it, so no name runs over the tile above, and in three columns the disk tile takes two cells so its list shows.
